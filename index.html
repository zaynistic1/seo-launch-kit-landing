Look under the hood first

This is a real excerpt from the IndexNow action inside the kit — the exact automation that submits changed pages on every push:

# .github/workflows/indexnow-auto-submit.yml
on:
  push:
    branches: [main, master]

jobs:
  indexnow:
    runs-on: ubuntu-latest
    steps:
      - uses: actions/checkout@v4
      # …builds the URL list from changed *.html files…
      - name: Submit URLs to IndexNow
        env:
          INDEXNOW_KEY: ${{ secrets.INDEXNOW_KEY }}
        run: |
          curl --fail-with-body --silent \
            --request POST "https://api.indexnow.org/indexnow" \
            --data "$payload"
